Privacy policy

WeGoal, by Lokko Labs LLC. Effective 29 September 2026.

What WeGoal collects

The medicine you tell us about, your doses and injection sites, your weight, meals, water, side effects, goals and the targets you set. If you sign in with Apple, a user id and the email address Apple shares with us, which can be a private relay address. Usage events such as "dose logged" or "paywall viewed", which never contain health values.

Where it is stored

On your iPhone. If you sign in, also in WeGoal's own database on Google Firebase in the United States, so a new phone or a reinstall never loses your history. Your health data is never stored in iCloud and never sold.

AI processing

Only when you allow it in the app, meal photos, nutrition-label photos and typed meal descriptions are sent through WeGoal's server to OpenAI to identify the foods. Nothing else is sent: not your name, medicine, doses, weight or side effects. Nutrient values come from the USDA food database where it has a match. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

Apple Health

Only when you turn it on, WeGoal writes your weight, protein and water to Apple Health and reads your weight from it. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing and never shared with an advertising platform.

Ideas and feedback

If you post an idea or vote on the in-app board, the text you write is visible to other signed-in WeGoal users. Your name and email are not shown.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, database, crash reports and usage analytics.
  • OpenAI: reading meal photos, labels and typed meals, only when you allow it.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status, with an anonymous app user id.
  • Open Food Facts: barcode lookups. Only the barcode is sent.

WeGoal contains no advertising SDKs.

Your choices

Export all your data as a spreadsheet or a PDF report from Settings. Delete your account and all server data from Settings at any time; the data on your phone is deleted with it. To stop AI processing or Apple Health sharing, turn them off in Settings.

Children

WeGoal is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.
