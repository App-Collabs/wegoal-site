WeGoal support

WeGoal tracks your GLP-1 doses, side effects, protein and water, and helps you keep your weight off after you stop.

Contact

Email support@lokkolabs.xyz. We answer within two business days. You can also shake your phone in the app to send an idea or report a bug.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To cancel, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then WeGoal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

In the app, Settings lets you export everything as a spreadsheet or a report for your doctor, and delete your account and all of its data. See the privacy policy.

Medical note

WeGoal is not medical advice. It does not calculate or recommend doses. Follow your prescriber, and talk to your doctor before you change your medicine.
